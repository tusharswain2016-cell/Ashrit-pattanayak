"My purpose is to serve every family in Pipili — their prosperity and welfare is my only mission."

Shri Ashrit Pattanayak is the elected Member of Legislative Assembly from the Pipili constituency in Puri district, Odisha. A dedicated public servant, he has committed himself to holistic development — focusing on infrastructure, education, healthcare, and farmer welfare across every corner of the constituency.

Pipili, renowned across the world for its vibrant traditional appliqué craft, is a constituency with deep cultural roots and immense untapped potential. Shri Pattanayak has worked to bridge the gap between tradition and modern development, ensuring artisans, farmers, and youth of Pipili have access to new opportunities and government welfare schemes.

With a strong grassroots connect and an unwavering vision for an inclusive, prosperous Pipili, he continues to be the voice of the people in the Odisha Legislative Assembly — raising issues that matter and delivering on the ground.

Vision & Agenda

Building a Better Pipili

Six development pillars guiding every initiative for the people of Pipili constituency.

01🌾
Farmer Welfare

Ensuring fair prices, irrigation access, and full government scheme benefits for every farming family across Pipili.

02🧵
Artisan & Craft Support

Preserving Pipili's world-renowned appliqué heritage while creating new markets, exports, and income security for artisans.

03📚
Education for All

Improving school infrastructure and ensuring every child in Pipili has access to quality education and scholarship support.

04🏥
Healthcare Access

Strengthening primary health centres, regular medical camps, and accessible affordable healthcare for every family.

05🛣️
Roads & Infrastructure

Connecting every village with all-weather roads, electricity, clean water supply, and modern civic amenities.

06🤝
Youth Empowerment

Skill development, employment, and entrepreneurship opportunities for the energetic youth of Pipili constituency.
